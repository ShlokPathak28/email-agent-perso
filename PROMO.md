> • Gmail & Outlook API support
> • Smart cleaning (HTML strip, signatures removed, tracking pixels gone)

> • Thread aggregation (no duplicate summaries)
> • Telegram delivery with chunking for long digests
> • Read-only — never modifies your inbox
>
> **Tech:** Python, Google Gmail API, MS Graph API, Telegram Bot API. The core logic was built with Kimi K2.6.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Maximum Reach

1. **Post at the right time:**
   - Twitter/X: 9am-11am EST
   - LinkedIn: Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-10am EST
   - Reddit: Weekday evenings (US time)
   - HN: 7am-10am PST

2. **Engage in the first hour** — reply to every comment. Algorithm loves it.

3. **Post a demo/screenshot** — People scroll past text. A Telegram screenshot with the digest gets 3x more engagement.

4. **Cross-post** — Post on Twitter, screenshot the tweet, post on LinkedIn with "People on Twitter loved this, so sharing here too."

5. **Tag relevant accounts** — @KimiAI (if they have a Twitter), @ProductHunt, @github
